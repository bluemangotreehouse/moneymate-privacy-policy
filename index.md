This policy describes how MoneyMate handles information. Some features described below are planned and are not currently available. The sections labeled **Planned features** apply only after those features are released and enabled in the app. This document must be reviewed and updated to match the actual implementation before those features launch.

## Current app behavior

MoneyMate currently stores transactions, categories, budgets, and related finance information in a database on your device. The app does not currently require an account, include Google Sign-In or Google Drive backup, or display AdMob ads. MoneyMate does not currently send this financial data to a MoneyMate-operated server.

If you choose to export or share information using your device's share or file tools, the destination you select receives the information you share. That destination's own privacy practices apply. Device operating-system backup settings may also affect how app data is backed up.

## Information stored on your device

The information you enter can include transaction amounts, dates, notes, categories, budgets, and other finance details supported by the app. This information is used to provide the app's budgeting, transaction, and reporting features. It remains on your device unless you choose to export or share it, or use a future cloud-backup feature described below.

You can remove locally stored information using the app's available controls or by uninstalling the app. Device backup and restore settings may affect whether data is retained outside the app installation.

## Planned features

The following practices describe the intended design for planned features. They are not a statement that these features are already active. This policy should be revised if the final implementation differs.

### Google Sign-In

If Google Sign-In is introduced, you may choose to sign in with a Google account. MoneyMate will receive the account information and authorization needed to complete sign-in, such as a Google account identifier and basic profile details made available by the permissions shown during sign-in. MoneyMate will not receive your Google password. Sign-in information will be used to identify your account and provide account-related backup and restore features.

You can disconnect Google Sign-In through the app, if provided, and revoke MoneyMate's access through your Google Account security settings. Disconnecting access may prevent cloud backup or restore from working.

### Google Drive backup and restore

If Google Drive backup is introduced and you choose to use it, MoneyMate will transfer the backup you request to storage associated with your Google account and retrieve it when you request a restore. A backup may contain the finance information listed above. The app should request only the Google Drive access necessary to create, read, update, and delete MoneyMate backup data; it should not access unrelated Drive files unless a future feature explicitly requires broader access and explains that request to you.

Cloud backups remain in your Google account until deleted. You will be able to remove a backup through the app if that control is provided, or through the applicable Google account controls. Revoking Drive access does not necessarily delete backups already stored in your account. Google handles its services under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

### AdMob advertising

If AdMob ads are introduced, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, approximate location inferred from IP address, diagnostic information, and ad delivery or measurement events, as permitted by your device settings, applicable law, and your consent choices. This information may be used to deliver, limit, personalize, and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

Ad personalization and advertising identifier controls may be available in your device settings and Google account. Where required, MoneyMate will request consent before using data for advertising and will provide legally required choices. Google's privacy policy and the controls presented in the app or device settings explain Google's processing in more detail.

## Sharing and service providers

MoneyMate does not sell personal information. Today, MoneyMate does not operate a server that receives your finance records. If the planned features are released, information will be shared only as needed to provide the feature you choose: Google Sign-In and Drive will use Google services, and ads may be provided and measured by Google AdMob and its partners. Google may process information under its own privacy terms. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

## Retention and deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, remove the app, or device backup settings affect it. If cloud backup is added, backups will remain in your Google account until you delete them. Any retention period for account or advertising data handled by Google is governed by Google's policies and settings. MoneyMate will update this section if it introduces server-side storage or other retention practices.

## Security

MoneyMate uses the storage and security protections available on your device. Planned cloud features should use encrypted connections and secure handling of account authorization. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, and MoneyMate cannot guarantee the security of information after it is shared with a third party or stored by Google.

## Children's privacy

MoneyMate is a personal finance tool and is not designed for children under 13. MoneyMate does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to MoneyMate, contact us using the address above.

## Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose what information to enter, whether to export or share it, and whether to use future optional account, backup, or advertising features. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information. Contact us using the address above to ask about a privacy request. Some information may remain in device or Google backups until those copies are separately removed.

## International processing

If future Google services are used, information may be processed in countries where Google or its service providers operate. The applicable service terms and privacy information describe Google's handling of that information.
